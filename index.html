    A small studio with a big engine

    The Graphite Mind is an independent studio with one founder and no outside funding yet. We started in September 2026. We make animated explainers for viewers in the United States: history, science, psychology and health so far, and in time any subject worth explaining.

    Every frame is drawn by Python code written with Claude Code. Our shared engine has about 33,000 lines of Python, 314 drawable props, 97 character designs and 72 animals and plants. Each new video reuses that engine and improves it, so each video gets cheaper and better to make.

    1. Today

      Eight long videos and seven Shorts are live as of October 2026, with subtitles in English and 8 other languages.

    2. Next 6 months

      We plan weekly long videos, dubbed tracks in Spanish, Portuguese and Hindi, and an open character kit as a reusable library.

    3. Next 12 months

      We plan localized channels, classroom versions with short quizzes, and a faster pipeline so one person can ship more videos without losing accuracy.
