Zee does the doubting.

Zee is our Gen Z skeptic, in a yellow hoodie, with a question for every big claim. When the narrator says something impressive, Zee wants to see the proof.

We have drawn Zee at four ages, from a kid with a backpack to an old man with glasses. It is the same function each time, with different settings.

Meet the cast

Zee is a function.

The code below is real. It is the part of our drawing engine that draws Zee’s eyes, brows and mouth, copied from the file. One short block is left out and marked.

# the-graphite-mind/longform/engine/cast.py, lines 299-321
# (inside figure(): eyes, brows, cheeks and mouth; lines 309-310 left out)
    ex = r * 0.36
    ey = cy - r * 0.02
    closed = blink or mood == "sleepy"
    for side in (-1, 1):
        ecx = cx + side * ex + look[0]
        ecy = ey + look[1]
        if closed:
            d.line(catmull([(ecx - 10, ecy), (ecx, ecy + 6), (ecx + 10, ecy)], 5), LW)
        else:
            ry = 14 if mood != "shocked" else 17
            # ...
            d.ellipse(ecx, ecy, 9 if mood != "shocked" else 11, ry, INK, outline=False)
            d.dot((ecx - 3, ecy - 5), 3.4, C["white"])
        bp = BROWS.get(mood, BROWS["neutral"])
        pts = [(ecx - side * x * 0.9 - look[0] * 0.3, ecy + y * 0.9) for x, y in bp]
        d.line(catmull(pts, 5), LW * 0.85)
    if mood in ("happy", "laugh", "proud"):
        for side in (-1, 1):
            d.ellipse(cx + side * r * 0.6, cy + r * 0.3, 11, 7, C["pink"], outline=False)
    if mood == "worried":
        d.fill(ellipse_pts(cx + r * 0.85, cy - r * 0.55, 6, 9) , C["sky"], width=2.5)
    _mouth(d, mood, cx, cy, r, braces="braces" in extras)

How it’s made

Who makes this

The Graphite Mind is a YouTube channel of animated explainers about history, science, psychology and health, made for viewers in the United States. The first video went up in September 2026.

A person picks every topic, reads every script, reviews every finished video and uploads it. Claude Code does the research notes, the scripts and all of the drawing code.

If we got something wrong, tell us. Questions, corrections, business and press all go to one address.
