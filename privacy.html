Privacy.

Short version: this site does not track you. Last updated October 7, 2026.

What this site does

  • It sets no cookies.
  • It runs no analytics and no advertising or tracking scripts.
  • It has no forms, no accounts and no comments, so it collects nothing you type.
  • It loads its fonts from its own server, not from Google or anyone else.
  • It does not embed YouTube players or any other third-party content. Thumbnails and covers are copies stored on this site.

What the host sees

The site is served as static files from GitHub Pages. When your browser loads a page, the host receives your IP address and the usual request details, as any web host does. That happens under GitHub’s own privacy statement. We do not receive that data and do not use it.

Links to YouTube

Videos live on YouTube. When you follow a link to youtube.com you leave this site, and what YouTube collects is governed by YouTube’s terms and Google’s privacy policy, not this page.

Email

If you write to henry@thegraphitemind.com, we get your email address and whatever you put in the message. We use it to reply and for nothing else, and we do not share it.

Questions about this notice go to the same address.
