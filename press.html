About us in 25, 50 and 100 words

25 words

The Graphite Mind is a YouTube channel of animated explainers on history, science, psychology and health, drawn by code for curious viewers in the US.

50 words

The Graphite Mind is a bootstrapped studio making animated explainers on history, science, psychology and health for US viewers on YouTube. We started in September 2026 with one founder and no outside funding. Every frame is drawn by Python code that Claude Code writes. A person picks the topics and reviews each video.

100 words

The Graphite Mind is a YouTube channel of animated explainers on history, science, psychology and health. It is made for curious viewers in the United States. The studio is independent and bootstrapped, with no outside funding yet, and it has one founder. It started in September 2026. As of October 7, 2026, it has published 8 long videos, from 6 to 27 minutes, and 7 Shorts. Every frame is drawn by Python code written with Claude Code, Anthropic’s coding tool. Claude also helps with research, fact-checking, scripts, subtitle translation and thumbnails. A person picks the topics, reviews each video and publishes it.
